Privacy Policy

Shorts Auto Poster is a personal, single-user automation tool. It runs locally on the owner's own computer and does not collect, store, or share personal data belonging to any third party.

The tool uses TikTok's official Content Posting API, with the account owner's explicit authorization (OAuth 2.0), solely to publish video content to the owner's own TikTok account. Access tokens are stored locally on the owner's device and are never transmitted to any third party or external server.

No data collected through the TikTok API is used for any purpose other than publishing the owner's own content to their own account.
